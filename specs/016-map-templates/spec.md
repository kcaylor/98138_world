## Context

Constitution 3.0.0, Principle I ("Templates Fix the Canvas; the Kit Is the Reference") lets maps be
built on **templates** other than the LEGO 31203 kit. A template is a projection; a rectangular
board of whole 16×16 modules at the kit's 8 mm stud pitch; a layout that assigns every stud to
**land**, **ocean** or **margin** (off the globe); one part type per class; and a frame. The kit
template (128×80 studs, LEGO's land/sea mask of 3,062 land and 7,178 ocean studs, the kit's fitted
equidistant cylindrical projection, its frame and combinations A/B/C) never changes and stays the
default. A new template's layout is generated by a maintainer tool from pinned, cited data and a
published projection definition, is reproducible with a `--check` run, and is confirmed by the
owner before it ships.

The projection study (`cli/tools/projection_study/`, README there) found that **Winkel tripel**
(the projection used for world maps by the National Geographic Society since 1998; standard
parallel 50.4598°, central meridian 11.1° E, the centre of the kit as hung in combination A) has a
width-to-height ratio of 1.637 against the kit board's 1.600, so it fits the kit's own 8 × 5
modules and frame:

| Quantity (ETOPO 2022, 4 × 4 samples per stud) | Winkel tripel on 8 × 5 | Kit template |
|---|---|---|
| Land / ocean / margin studs | 2,554 / 5,706 / 1,980 | 3,062 / 7,178 / 0 (LEGO's mask) |
| One-stud outline ring (part of the margin) | 286 | — |
| Modules fully on the globe / partly / not at all | 18 / 22 / 0 | 40 / 0 / 0 |
| Largest clear block in each corner, inside the ring | 15 × 13 studs | — |
| Ground area per stud, 90th / 10th percentile | 1.90 | 4.68 |

The same data method applied to the kit grid agrees with LEGO's mask on 94.2 % of studs, which is
why a data-derived layout needs the owner's review before it ships.

**Terms.** *Template*: as above, named by an identifier (`kit-31203`, `winkel-tripel`).
*Margin*: studs off the globe, built from the margin part. *Outline ring*: margin studs that touch
an on-globe stud along an edge. *Confirmed*: a template whose data file records the owner's
confirmation; only confirmed templates can be built.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-11-23

- Q: How does a layout record its template without changing kit layouts? → A: `metadata.template`
  is written only for a non-kit template; a layout without it is the kit template (auto-resolved:
  the same pattern as `metadata.arrangement`, absent = A, feature 007; `layout/grid.py`).
- Q: Are the new recipe keys and command-line options additive? → A: yes: an optional `template`
  key and an optional margin section (margin color, ring color), with matching optional
  `map build` options; every existing recipe, flag and file keeps its meaning (auto-resolved:
  feature 009 added `arrangement` the same way; constitution II "A recipe MAY name a confirmed
  template").
- Q: How are hand corrections and the owner's confirmation recorded? → A: in the template data
  file: each correction as row, column, new class and reason (the `tools/kit31203/overrides.json`
  pattern); the confirmation as the role "project owner" and a date, with no personal name
  (auto-resolved: overrides pattern; personal data stays out of committed files, run policy).
- Q: How are inland basins below sea level (Caspian Sea, Dead Sea, Qattara Depression) classified?
  → A: by the study rule (elevation above 0 is land), so the Caspian Sea is water; the review sheet
  flags below-sea-level studs not connected to the ocean, and studs with a land share between 40 %
  and 60 %, for the owner's review (auto-resolved: the rule reproduced the kit mask on 94.2 % of
  studs; Principle I requires the review).
- Q: Where do the square tile's colors come from? → A: the part-color maintainer tool adds part
  3070b from the already pinned Rebrickable elements snapshot plus a pinned BrickLink catalog page
  for item 3070b, with the same rules as the round plate and tile (auto-resolved:
  `tools/partcolors/build.py`, feature 005 and the part-color snapshots).
- Q: Should `official-31203` (LEGO's design copied cell for cell) get a Winkel version? → A: no;
  it stays kit-only, and building it on another template stops with an error suggesting
  `official-31203-bathymetry` (user).
- Q: Which fit of Winkel tripel to the board? → A: the projection's exact published extent,
  x = ±(π/2)(1 + cos φ1), y = ±π/2. The study's counts in this spec (2,554 / 5,706 / 1,980, ring
  286, corners 15 × 13, leftovers 510 and 2,360) came from a sampled extent that missed the
  antimeridian and are approximate; the exact fit gives 2,553 / 5,691 / 1,996, ring 288, corners
  14 × 14, leftovers 511 and 2,375. Acceptance uses the confirmed template's counts (agent
  decision during planning: research.md R2).
- Q: FR-012 asks for round and square studs in previews, but kit previews draw one square per stud
  and SC-001 keeps them unchanged. → A: the shapes apply to non-kit previews only; kit previews
  stay as they are, and FR-013's template naming likewise applies to non-kit reports (agent
  decision during planning: research.md R13).
- Q: Main now draws kit previews as round studs on black (commit 6038d9c, owner). Do template
  previews keep their own style? → A: No. Every map preview, kit and template, draws round studs
  on black in main's style; on a template the margin studs are squares in the margin or ring
  color, so FR-012 holds, and kit previews match main byte for byte (SC-001 against the re-based
  golden) (agent, 2026-11-23: main 6038d9c; research.md R13 note).
- Q: Are margin labels built from square tiles? → A: No. Labels become a printed sticker on a black
  tile, in feature 017 (map stickers), which can also place the plaque in a Winkel margin corner
  after 016 ships; 016's margin is a color and an optional outline ring (user).
- Q: BrickLink's catalog page for item 3070b now redirects; which item do the pinned page and the
  exports use? → A: BrickLink item 3070, which lists 3070b as an alternate number (as part 6141
  is BrickLink item 4073); Rebrickable stays 3070b. `part_numbers.json` records both, and exports
  and price lots take the BrickLink number from it (agent, implementation finding: T010).

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Build a map on the Winkel tripel template (Priority: P1)

A maker builds any suitable bundled recipe on the Winkel tripel template from the command line and
gets a layout, preview, report, parts list, marketplace exports and build sheets for a map whose
curved world sits inside the kit's rectangular board, with the margin filled in flat square tiles.

**Why this priority**: it is the outcome the owner asked for: a different projection that still
hangs in the kit's frame as a piece of art.

**Independent Test**: with a small committed fixture template (and with the real Winkel template),
build `ocean-sst` on the template; every on-globe stud carries a data color in its class's part,
every margin stud is a square tile in the margin color, the counts match the template's layout,
and the parts list, exports and build sheets list the square tiles.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the confirmed Winkel template, **When** the maker runs `map build ocean-sst
   --template winkel-tripel -o layout.json`, **Then** the layout has 10,240 positions: 2,554 land,
   5,706 ocean and 1,980 margin (or the confirmed layout's counts if review changed them), and it
   records the template.
2. **Given** a recipe with `template = "winkel-tripel"`, **When** it is built without
   `--template`, **Then** it is built on the Winkel template; **When** it is built with
   `--template kit-31203`, **Then** the flag wins and the kit template is used.
3. **Given** a recipe without a template key, **When** it is built without `--template`, **Then**
   the layout, preview, report, parts list, exports and sheets are byte-for-byte identical to
   today's.
4. **Given** a recipe that sets no margin treatment, **When** it is built on the Winkel template,
   **Then** every margin stud is a Black 1×1 square tile and there is no outline ring.
5. **Given** a recipe (or command-line options) setting a margin color and an outline-ring color,
   **When** it is built, **Then** the 286 ring studs take the ring color and the other margin
   studs take the margin color.
6. **Given** a Winkel layout, **When** the maker asks for the parts list, **Then** it lists the
   land plates, ocean tiles and square tiles with cited LEGO, BrickLink and Rebrickable IDs per
   color; the one-kit top-up counts every square tile as needed beyond the kit.
7. **Given** a Winkel layout, **When** the maker builds sheets, **Then** the 40 section pages draw
   margin studs as squares with their color numbers and per-section counts include them.

### User Story 2 - Generate, check and confirm a template (Priority: P1)

A maintainer generates the Winkel template's layout from the pinned elevation dataset and the
projection's published definition, reviews it on a review sheet against the data, records hand
corrections and the owner's confirmation in the template's data file, and can later prove with a
`--check` run that the committed layout is reproducible.

**Why this priority**: no template can ship without it (Principle I), and the owner asked to review
data-derived layouts.

**Independent Test**: run the maintainer tool on the cached dataset; it writes a candidate template
and a review sheet outside the repository; after confirmation is recorded, `--check` reports "no
differences" against the committed file, and changing one committed stud makes `--check` fail.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the cached ETOPO 2022 dataset, **When** the maintainer generates `winkel-tripel`,
   **Then** the candidate layout classifies each stud as land, ocean or margin with the study's
   method and records the projection definition, the board, the dataset's identity and checksum,
   the method and the generation date.
2. **Given** a candidate layout, **When** the maintainer asks for a review sheet, **Then** it shows
   every stud by class, the outline ring, the module grid and section names, and highlights studs
   whose land share was close to the threshold.
3. **Given** hand corrections (stud, new class, reason) and a confirmation (who, date), **When**
   the maintainer writes the template, **Then** the data file records them, and **When** they run
   `--check`, **Then** the regenerated layout with the corrections applied equals the committed one.
4. **Given** a template file without a confirmation, **When** a maker tries to build on it,
   **Then** the build stops with an error that names the template as unconfirmed.

### User Story 3 - Winkel versions on the web site (Priority: P2)

A visitor to 98138.world sees, for each bundled map that can be built on it, a Winkel tripel
version beside combinations A, B and C, with its preview, parts summary, leftovers, price estimate
where the site shows one, the palette chooser, and every build file.

**Why this priority**: the site is how most people see the maps, but the CLI comes first
(Principle VI).

**Independent Test**: run the web prebuild offline with fixtures; the manifest lists a Winkel
variant for each eligible bundled map with checksummed files, the map page links to it, and the
kit variants are byte-for-byte unchanged.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the bundled maps, **When** the site is prebuilt, **Then** a Winkel variant exists for
   `official-31203-bathymetry`, `ocean-sst`, `land-elevation`, `living-planet` and `rainfall`, and
   none for `official-31203` (kit-only) or the Pacific-centred `ocean-el-nino` and `ocean-la-nina`.
2. **Given** a map page, **When** the visitor chooses the Winkel version, **Then** the page shows
   its preview with square margin studs, its piece counts including square tiles, and its
   downloads, and says the frames cannot be re-hung for this version.
3. **Given** the site without JavaScript, **When** the visitor opens a Winkel variant, **Then**
   every preview and download still works.
4. **Given** a Winkel variant with JavaScript, **When** the visitor uses "Try other colors",
   **Then** land and ocean classes recolor as on kit variants, margin studs stay square in the
   margin color, and "show ice as White" covers the template's own ice regions.

### Edge Cases

- A recipe or flag names an unknown template, or a template without the owner's confirmation:
  error listing the confirmed templates.
- A non-kit template with an arrangement other than A, set by the recipe (the ENSO recipes set B)
  or by `--arrangement`: error saying the frames of a curved map cannot be re-hung. `layout
  arrange` on a non-kit layout: the same error.
- `official-31203` (the design copied cell for cell from the kit grid) on a non-kit template:
  error suggesting `official-31203-bathymetry`, which gives the official look from data.
- The land shadow and coastal halo near the outline: they never place a color on a margin stud,
  and a land stud whose eastern neighbour is margin casts no shadow.
- Geographic rules (ice regions, region overrides, unobserved-latitude rules, land/ocean splits
  from another dataset) on a non-kit template: they use each stud's own location in that template,
  so the same place gets the same rule on every template.
- Data cells with no value under a non-kit template: the recipe's existing no-data rules apply.
- A price snapshot without the square tile: the Winkel estimate is marked incomplete with the
  unpriced tile colors listed; kit variants are unaffected.
- A margin or ring color not available for the square tile, or the same color for both: error
  naming the available colors.
- A layout file without a template field: read as the kit template.
- Suitability: the check runs against the template's own grid, so its scale figures reflect the
  template.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

**Templates**

- **FR-001**: The system MUST define a template as an identifier, title, projection definition,
  board size in whole 16×16 modules, per-stud class (land, ocean, margin), one part per class,
  frame, and confirmation record, stored as versioned package data with a cited source.
- **FR-002**: The kit template (`kit-31203`) MUST be the default and MUST reproduce today's grid,
  mask, projection, frame and arrangements exactly.
- **FR-003**: The system MUST ship one new template, `winkel-tripel`: Winkel tripel with standard
  parallel 50.4598° and central meridian 11.1° E, fitted to the kit's 8 × 5 modules and frame.
- **FR-004**: Only templates whose data file records the owner's confirmation MUST be buildable.
- **FR-005**: For each template the system MUST give the geographic location of every on-globe
  stud and find the stud that contains a given location, so data can be sampled onto any template.

**Building**

- **FR-006**: `map build` MUST accept a template from the recipe (optional `template` key) or the
  command line; the command line wins; with neither, the kit template is used. Recipe keys and
  options added by this feature MUST be optional, and existing recipes MUST keep their meaning.
- **FR-007**: On a non-kit template, data MUST be sampled at each on-globe stud's location; land
  studs take the land layer and ocean studs the ocean layer; margin studs never take data.
- **FR-008**: Classification, color scales, texture, the land shadow, the coastal halo, ice and
  region rules MUST work on non-kit templates as they do on the kit, with neighbours taken on the
  template's board and never across the margin.
- **FR-009**: Margin studs MUST use the 1×1 square tile (LEGO part 3070b) in one margin color, with
  an optional outline ring in a second color; the default is Black with no ring. Colors MUST be
  chosen from the tile's cited colors.
- **FR-010**: The layout MUST record the template (as `metadata.template`, written only for a
  non-kit template) and each stud's class and part; layouts without it MUST be read as the kit
  template.
- **FR-011**: A non-kit template with an arrangement other than A, `layout arrange` on a non-kit
  layout, and `official-31203` on a non-kit template MUST stop with the errors in Edge Cases.
- **FR-012**: Previews MUST draw margin studs as squares and land and ocean studs as rounds.
- **FR-013**: Reports and the suitability check MUST name the template and give its counts.

**Parts, sheets and validation**

- **FR-014**: Parts lists, BrickLink and Rebrickable exports, the one-kit top-up, leftovers and
  price estimates MUST include the square tiles with cited LEGO, BrickLink and
  Rebrickable IDs and colors from the same checksum-pinned catalog snapshots as the existing part
  colors (the part-color maintainer tool adds part 3070b, with a pinned BrickLink catalog page for
  item 3070b).
- **FR-015**: Build sheets MUST draw margin studs as squares with color numbers; the legend MUST
  list the square tile's colors; per-section counts MUST include them.
- **FR-016**: Layout validation MUST check a layout against its own template's classes and parts.

**Maintainer tool**

- **FR-017**: A maintainer tool MUST generate a template's layout from the pinned ETOPO 2022
  dataset and the projection definition (4 × 4 samples per stud; a stud is on the globe when at
  least half its samples are; land when at least half of those have elevation above 0), produce a
  review sheet outside the repository that flags below-sea-level studs not connected to the ocean
  and studs with a land share between 40 % and 60 %, apply recorded hand corrections, record the
  confirmation, and support `--check` (exit 0 and "no differences" when the committed file is
  reproducible).
- **FR-018**: The template data file MUST record the dataset identity and checksum, the projection
  definition, the method and thresholds, each hand correction (row, column, class, reason), and
  the confirmation (the role "project owner" and a date; no personal name).

**Web (P2)**

- **FR-019**: The web prebuild MUST build a Winkel variant of each eligible bundled map (US3) with
  the same files as the kit variants, list them in the manifest, and leave every kit variant's
  files byte-for-byte unchanged.
- **FR-020**: Map pages MUST offer the Winkel version beside A, B and C and state that it cannot be
  re-hung; pages MUST work without JavaScript.
- **FR-020a**: The per-cell export for the palette chooser (`map cells`) MUST cover non-kit
  templates: it marks margin cells, which the chooser draws as squares and never recolors as land
  or ocean, and it carries the template's ice regions.
- **FR-020b**: Each template MUST have its own ice regions, generated by a maintainer tool with the
  same rules as the kit's (`data/kit_31203_ice.json`: land ice south of 60° S and in the Greenland
  box; sea ice from ERSST v5 1991–2020) and reproducible with `--check`.

**Labels**

- **FR-021**: (Removed 2026-11-23: margin labels moved to feature 017, map stickers.)

**Compliance**

- **FR-022**: Every new user-facing output MUST carry "Not affiliated with or endorsed by The LEGO
  Group."

### Key Entities

- **Template**: identifier, title, projection definition, board (modules across and down), per-stud
  class, part per class, frame, source (dataset, method), corrections, confirmation.
- **Margin treatment**: margin color and optional outline-ring color; chosen by the recipe or
  command line.
- **Template correction**: a stud, the class it is changed to, and the reviewer's reason.
- **Layout (extended)**: today's layout plus the template identifier and the margin class.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: Every bundled recipe built on the kit template produces outputs byte-for-byte
  identical to those before this feature.
- **SC-002**: Five bundled maps build on the Winkel template, and each one's land, ocean and margin
  counts equal the confirmed template's.
- **SC-003**: A Winkel map's parts list is complete: the sum of its parts equals 10,240 studs, and
  every color has cited LEGO, BrickLink and Rebrickable IDs.
- **SC-004**: The Winkel build uses the kit's 40 modules and frame unchanged, and its land plates
  and ocean tiles each total no more than one kit holds (3,064 and 8,066).
- **SC-005**: The maintainer tool's `--check` reports "no differences" for the committed Winkel
  template on a machine with the cached dataset.
- **SC-006**: The web site offers a Winkel version for the five eligible maps, and every kit
  variant's files are unchanged.
- **SC-007**: All tests run offline.

## Assumptions

- The board stays 8 × 5 modules; Equal Earth, Mollweide and Robinson on 8 × 4 or 10 × 5, Spilhaus
  on 6 × 6 and Patterson on 9 × 5 are on the roadmap (`docs/vision.md`), not in this feature.
- The default margin is Black square tiles with no ring; the base bricks of the kit are black, so
  a black margin reads as a mat around the map. A recipe can choose other colors.
- Land and ocean for the Winkel template come from ETOPO 2022 elevation (above 0 is land), the
  dataset the kit projection was fitted with; the owner's review can correct individual studs.
- The kit's frame and its 40 modules work unchanged for any 8 × 5 template, so no new frame data is
  needed.
- The Winkel template is centred on 11.1° E. A Pacific-centred Winkel template may follow with its
  own review.
- `official-31203` stays kit-only (owner, 2026-11-23): it copies LEGO's design cell for cell rather
  than sampling data.
- Price estimates (011), leftovers (014) and the palette chooser (015) are on the main line. The
  square tile is priced like any other part once the BrickLink price snapshot has rows for it;
  refreshing the snapshot needs the owner's BrickLink credentials, so until then a Winkel estimate
  is marked incomplete, as 011 does for any unpriced part. Leftovers count kit elements only: the
  square tiles add no leftover rows, and a Winkel map leaves more kit plates and tiles in the box
  (with one kit, 510 White plates and at least 2,360 tiles, depending on the colors used).
- The projection math needed at run time (forward and inverse Winkel tripel) is published and
  short; whether it comes from a new run-time dependency is a planning decision.
- Out of scope: other board sizes, shaped boards, interrupted or square projections, a map planner
  interface, re-hanging non-kit templates, and labels or plaques in the margin (feature 017, map
  stickers, after this feature ships).
